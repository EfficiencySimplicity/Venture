### Main Idea:

- You are on a peninsula, bounded by cliffs. 
- Every time you die (this is a time loop game) you wake up in a little cabin.
- Some things you do in one life persist across time (maybe not forever, tho)
    - You will need to die multiple times and do specific things each time to escape
- The goal of the game is to escape via getting over the cliffs.
    - Possibly boat around as alt ending? Multiple endings?
        - Or sea is too harsh to traverse
